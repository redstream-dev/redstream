# rdsanalytics config for rds-test3 (--analytics-config) on the street clip (tests/models/fetch-tracker-clip.sh: a
# diagonal crosswalk, people walking east and west, cars driving east). Ours, written for that clip in the format of
# DeepStream's nvdsanalytics configs; the coordinates are in a 1280x720 config space, scaled to the mux output.
#
# Each stream has the same three rules, so up to four copies of the clip (sources 0-3) count alike:
# - roi-Crosswalk: the people (class 0) on the near half of the street, in the middle of the frame;
# - line-crossing-Eastbound: people and cars (classes 0 and 2) crossing the vertical line x = 640, from y 380 down
#   to the bottom of the frame, going east (the direction vector points right);
# - direction-West: people going west.
# An object stands for its box's bottom centre. osd-mode=2 draws the rules, the counts and each object's status.

enable=1
config-width=1280
config-height=720
osd-mode=2
display-font-size=12
